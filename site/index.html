Notchisland

Your MacBook notch, made useful. Coding agents, music, your day and your calls — one click from the top of the screen.

Download for Mac View on GitHub

Free and open source · macOS 13 or later · Apple Silicon and Intel

Coding agents

Your agents, live in the notch.

Claude Code, Codex and OpenCode sessions stay in view while you work elsewhere. See what each one is doing, how long it has left, and step in the second one needs you.

Live sessions

Every running agent with its project, current step, context and tokens.

Time left

Estimates learned from your recent turns, so you know whether to wait or walk away.

Approve from the notch

Tool permission requests drop down as a peek. Allow or deny without finding the terminal.

Usage limits

Session and weekly limits, context, and tokens by agent, model and session.

Know where your limits are

Read locally from Claude Code and Codex logs and OpenCode's database. Antigravity quotas are read live.

Works with the tools you use

Claude Code and desktop, Codex CLI, Cursor, Zed, OpenCode, Qoder, Gemini CLI — plus a webhook, socket and inbox for anything else.

Music

Whatever's playing.

A full player for Spotify and Apple Music, and for YouTube in your browser.

  • Scrubbable timeline, play, skip, shuffle and repeat.
  • YouTube in the browser shows as music, with its thumbnail and Up next.
  • Playlists and queue one click away.
  • Output devices and their format, switchable from the notch.

Planner

Your day, at the top of the screen.

Events from every calendar you've connected, reminders you can tick off, and quick notes.

  • Join a meeting straight from the notch, 15 minutes before it starts.
  • Reminders due soon, with a Done button.
  • Timers that count down beside the notch.

Calls

Calls ring in the notch.

From FaceTime, WhatsApp or a meeting in your browser — answer or decline without hunting for the window. Once you pick up, a running timer and a hang-up button stay in reach.

And the rest

Everything else, one click away.

Tray and clipboard shelf

Drag files onto the notch and back out anywhere, AirDrop them or reveal them in Finder. Press ⌃⌥C to shelve what's on the clipboard.

Weather and batteries

Conditions and the next hours, a heads-up before rain, and the battery of your Mac, AirPods and Magic accessories.

Notifications you can answer

Messages, Slack, Discord, WhatsApp and more — reply from the notch.

Glance on hover

Rest the pointer on the notch for what's playing, the weather and your battery. Move away and it's gone.

Install

Get Notchisland.

Homebrew

The quickest way, and it keeps you up to date.

brew install --cask AmdjedBelgacem/tap/notchisland

Download

Get the zip from the latest release, unzip it and move Notchisland to Applications.

Privacy

Everything stays on your Mac.

Local

Agent events, usage and notifications are read and kept on this Mac.

No account

Nothing to sign up for. Open it and it works.

No telemetry

No analytics and no tracking. The code is open, so you can check.
